<h2 class="research-title">Certified Dynamic Reconfiguration of TSN Networks (PhD)</h2>
<p class="research-desc"><a href="https://1.ieee802.org/tsn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Time-Sensitive Networking (TSN)</a> promises bounded latency, but measurements only show a lower bound on the worst-case delay. My PhD uses deterministic <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_calculus" target="_blank" rel="noopener">network calculus</a> to prove worst-case latency bounds and to keep them certified when the network is reconfigured at runtime. <a href="https://www.ece.fr/en/research-focuses-and-structuring" target="_blank" rel="noopener">LyRIDS</a> (ECE) and <a href="https://cedric.cnam.fr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CEDRIC</a> (Cnam). <a href="https://cnam-ece.github.io/learning-materials/blog/why-deterministic-guarantees" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Read the notes &rarr;</a></p>
</div>

<h2 class="research-title">Secure Genomic Data Sharing with Zero-Knowledge Proofs</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Blockchain-based platforms for sharing genomic data among multiple parties while protecting participants' identities. This work analyzes <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero-knowledge_proof" target="_blank" rel="noopener">zero-knowledge proof</a> (ZKP) based approaches to multi-party genomic data sharing and builds trust-based sharing and privacy-preserving aggregated biomedical analysis on top of them. COSMO team, IBISC, with Prof. Nazim Agoulmine and Dr. Adnan Imeri. <a href="https://univ-evry.hal.science/hal-05645365/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Read the article (ADVANCE 2026) &rarr;</a></p>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Selective Disclosure of Electronic Health Records</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Secure, traceable transmission of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_health_record" target="_blank" rel="noopener">electronic health records</a> on the <a href="https://sui.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Sui</a> blockchain, with contextual access control and privacy protection through zero-knowledge smart contracts (ZKContract). The resulting prototype guarantees confidentiality, integrity and traceability of medical data and won the Shaker Genopole Challenge.</p>
