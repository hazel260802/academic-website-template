I'm currently a PhD candidate in Computer Systems Networking and Telecommunications at the [Conservatoire national des arts et métiers](https://www.cnam.fr/) and [ECE Engineering School](https://www.ece.fr/) in Paris, within the [LyRIDS laboratory of ECE](https://www.ece.fr/en/research-focuses-and-structuring), the [CEDRIC laboratory of Cnam](https://cedric.cnam.fr/) and the SMI doctoral school.
My current research focuses on [Time-Sensitive Networking](https://1.ieee802.org/tsn/), deterministic [network calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_calculus) and the certified dynamic reconfiguration of TSN networks, as well as blockchain, [zero-knowledge proofs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero-knowledge_proof) and privacy-preserving systems for healthcare and genomic data.

## About me

Since November 2026, I have been preparing a PhD at [Cnam](https://www.cnam.fr/) and [ECE](https://www.ece.fr/) on the [certified dynamic reconfiguration of TSN networks]({{ '/about' | relative_url }}), which aims to keep provable worst-case latency guarantees while a network is reconfigured at runtime.
In 2026, I completed the [Master 2 in Computer and Network Systems, Network Systems track](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/en/education/master/computer-science/m2-computer-and-network-systems-cns-systemes-en-reseau-sr) at [Université d'Évry Paris-Saclay](https://www.univ-evry.fr/).
In 2024, I received my engineering degree in Computer Science from the [Vietnam–France Information Technology programme](https://soict.hust.edu.vn/en/?p=18332), part of the PFIEV elite programme at [Hanoi University of Science and Technology](https://hust.edu.vn/).

My research experience started at the [BKCS lab](https://bkcs.hust.edu.vn/) of HUST, where I worked with [Prof. Tran Hai-Anh](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=W9ya5mMAAAAJ) on multi-objective deep reinforcement learning for edge-cloud server selection, and [published the article](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10630895/) at [IEEE Cloud Summit 2024](https://www.computer.org/cloud-summit-2024).
In 2025, I did a 3-month research internship at the [Faculty of Engineering of Universidade do Porto](https://www.up.pt/feup/en/), funded by [Erasmus+](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/) and an [IDEX Paris-Saclay scholarship](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/admission/bourses-et-aides-financieres/bourses-de-stage-linternational-idex), working on context-aware location privacy for multimodal transportation with Dr. Mariana Cunha and [Prof. João P. Vilela](https://www.dcc.fc.up.pt/~joaovilela/), and presented a mini [poster](https://kdradjat.github.io/jdse-2025/posters/3_Context-Aware_Location_Privacy-Preserving.pdf) at [JDSE 2025](https://kdradjat.github.io/jdse-2025/).
From September 2025, I was a Master 2 research intern in the [COSMO team](https://www.ibisc.univ-evry.fr/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Plaquette_COSMO-18062020-2.pdf) at the [IBISC Laboratory](https://www.ibisc.univ-evry.fr/) under [Prof. Nazim Agoulmine](https://www.ibisc.univ-evry.fr/en/the-directors-foreword/), working on blockchain-based genomic data sharing with zero-knowledge proofs. This work was published as a [full article](https://univ-evry.hal.science/hal-05645365/) at [ADVANCE 2026](https://www.ibisc.univ-evry.fr/en/?p=14870) and presented as posters at the [P3S3 Summer School](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/en/events/privacy-protocols-proofs-saclay-summer-school-p3s3) and [ACM womENcourage 2026](https://womencourage.acm.org/2026/index.php/poster-session/), where our team also built [SafeProof](https://ttgenproject.github.io/safeproof-zkp/), a zero-knowledge harassment reporting network, at the hackathon.
I also led the [HOPPERS](https://llm-intent-orchestration.vercel.app) project at the [D4Gen Hackathon](https://genopole.agorize.com/en/challenges/d4gen-hackathon-2026), which secures the collection and transmission of electronic health records over [Software-Defined Networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software-defined_networking) emulated in [Mininet-WiFi](https://mininet-wifi.github.io/). Our team presented the [final pitch](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/17n0wptI4n4wjyPATJ44HPGJ8dE6MdHPh/edit?usp=sharing) and won the [Shaker Genopole Challenge](https://genopole.agorize.com/web/en/challenges/618/teams/2643/participation).
I co-authored [LLMThings](https://llmthings.github.io/demo/), which received the [Best Presentation Award](https://www.ibisc.univ-evry.fr/anais-sadoudi-et-huyen-trang-le-recoivent-le-best-presentation-award-a-la-jdse-2026-english-version-below/) at [JDSE 2026](https://jdse2026.github.io/program/).

Beyond research, I was President of the [HUST Finance and Technology Club](https://fintech.hust.edu.vn/get-involved/club), where I organized more than 10 workshops and events on blockchain and crypto-finance for 70 members, and led the extracurricular activities committee of the HUST Francophone Club.
With the [Agence universitaire de la Francophonie](https://www.auf.org/), I organized the [NestQuant 2023](https://hust.edu.vn/vi/news/khoa-hoc-cong-nghe-dmst/bach-khoa-ha-noi-khuyen-khich-sinh-vien-su-dung-cong-nghe-moi-654914.html) and [Chat'Innov AUF 2023](https://hust.edu.vn/en/news/news/the-1st-hackathon-competition-for-french-speaking-students-in-vietnam-on-chat-gpt-653901.html) programming competitions, which brought together more than 150 participants, as well as francophone career orientation events.
I also served as a [StartupCity ambassador](https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=2640232599461776&set=a.371740252977700) and represented [BK Fintech](https://fintech.hust.edu.vn/) at the [Korea–Vietnam Entrepreneurship Education Forum](https://hust.edu.vn/vi/news/khoa-hoc-cong-nghe-dmst/bach-khoa-ha-noi-tien-phong-trong-cong-nghe-va-kinh-te-so-654873.html).
